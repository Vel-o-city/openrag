# Halden Institute — Internal Memorandum

**To:** Research staff
**From:** Office of the Director
**Subject:** Partnership review and instrument standards

## Partnership review

The Institute's partnership with Meridian on the Kepler Initiative has been
reviewed and the Director recommends continuing it through the programme's
fifth year. Kepler remains the Institute's largest external collaboration.

Dr. Ingrid Halvorsen will continue as the Institute's co-chair. Staff should
route programme correspondence through her office rather than contacting
Meridian's Lisbon office directly.

## A note on history

Several staff have asked about the Institute's earlier connection to Meridian
Labs. Dr. Amara Okonkwo led the Institute's sensor group before leaving to
direct Meridian in 2021. The working relationship between the two
organisations predates the Kepler Initiative by some years, and the Institute
holds a non-exclusive licence to the Wavelet platform as a result.

## Instrument standards

Following the salinity drift reported by the Kepler team, the Institute is
adopting an annual recalibration requirement for all buoy-mounted instruments,
not only Wavelet units. Tomas Ferreira has agreed to chair the working group
drafting the standard.

The Institute will also begin logging calibration history centrally. Priya
Raghunathan has offered to share Meridian's calibration tooling, which the
working group is evaluating.

## Budget note

The Nordic Marine Council grant that funds the Institute's share of Kepler is
up for renewal. The Office of the Director expects a decision within the year.
Staff should not assume continuity of funding for programme-dependent work
beyond the current cycle.
